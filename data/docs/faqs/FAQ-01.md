## Q: How do I find trains between two cities?

Tell the assistant your origin and destination stations (station names
or common aliases like "Bombay" or "CST" are understood), the date you
want to travel, and optionally a time of day or class. The assistant
looks up matching trains from the timetable — it never guesses a train
number or time from memory.

Source: looked up live via the train-search tool, not a rules document.
