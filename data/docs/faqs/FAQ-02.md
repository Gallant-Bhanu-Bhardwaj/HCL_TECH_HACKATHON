## Q: How can I check seat availability before booking?

Ask for availability on a specific train, date and class (for example,
"Is there a 3AC seat on 12123 this Friday?"). The assistant checks
current seats, RAC and waitlist counts and tells you the outcome —
confirmed, RAC, waitlisted, or fully booked — it never estimates this.
