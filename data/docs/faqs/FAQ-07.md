## Q: What is the cancellation charge for 2AC if I cancel more than 2 days early?

**This answer is now out of date.** When this FAQ was written, the
charge for cancelling a 2AC ticket 48 hours or more before departure
was **Rs 150**, per RUL-CAN-01 §3. CIR-2026-07 (effective 2026-11-15)
revised this to **Rs 200**, and CIR-2026-11 (effective 2026-12-10)
revises it further to **Rs 250**. The assistant must always quote the
figure that is in effect at the time of the cancellation, from the
`settings` table — never this FAQ's original Rs 150 figure.

Source (original, now superseded): RUL-CAN-01, section "3. Refund
table (confirmed travellers)". Current figure: CIR-2026-07 and/or
CIR-2026-11, whichever is in effect.
