## Q: What is the Tatkal surcharge for 3AC?

**This answer is now out of date.** When this FAQ was written, the
3AC Tatkal surcharge was **Rs 300**, per RUL-TAT-01 §4. CIR-2026-01
(effective 2026-10-01) revised it to **Rs 350**. The assistant must
always quote the current `tatkal_charge_3AC` setting, not this
original figure.

Source (original, now superseded): RUL-TAT-01, section "4. Tatkal
surcharge". Current figure: CIR-2026-01.
