## Q: When do I get my refund if my train is cancelled?

If Ujjwal Rail cancels your train, every booking on it is refunded in
full automatically, with no cancellation charge — you do not need to
ask separately.

Source: RUL-OPC-01, section "2. Train cancelled by the operator".
