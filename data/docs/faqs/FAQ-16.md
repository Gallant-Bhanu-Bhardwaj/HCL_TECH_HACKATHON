## Q: How much luggage can I carry for free?

It depends on your class: 40 kg in SL/2S, 35 kg in 3AC/CC, 50 kg in
2AC/EC, and 70 kg in 1AC. Anything above that is booked separately as
parcel luggage at the station.

Source: RUL-LUG-01, section "2. Free luggage allowance".
