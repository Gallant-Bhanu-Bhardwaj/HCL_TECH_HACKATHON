## Q: How do I file a complaint about my journey?

Tell the assistant what happened and, if it relates to a booking, the
booking details. The assistant will pass it to the right desk
(complaints, refunds, safety, or concessions) with a short summary. It
will not promise a specific outcome — only staff at that desk decide
that.

Source: RUL-GRV-01, sections "2. Desks" and "3. What every hand-over includes".
