## 1. What changes

From **15 August 2026**, in addition to the physical originals listed
in RUL-ID-01 §2, Ujjwal Rail on-board staff also accept a digital copy
of an accepted ID shown on a passenger's phone, provided it is sourced
from the issuing authority's own official app or portal (for example,
a DigiLocker-issued Aadhaar or driving licence). A screenshot or a
plain photo of an ID document, not sourced this way, is **not**
accepted.

## 2. No change to which documents qualify

This circular only widens *how* an accepted ID may be shown — it does
not add or remove any document from the accepted list in RUL-ID-01 §2,
and the age-based rules in RUL-ID-01 §3 are unchanged.
