## 1. What changes

From **1 October 2026**, the Tatkal surcharge in RUL-TAT-01 §4 is
revised upward in every class that offers Tatkal:

| Class | Old surcharge (RUL-TAT-01) | New surcharge (this circular) |
|---|---|---|
| SL  | Rs 100 | Rs 120 |
| 3AC | Rs 300 | Rs 350 |
| 2AC | Rs 400 | Rs 450 |
| CC  | Rs 150 | Rs 180 |

These update the `tatkal_charge_<CLASS>` settings. Nothing else in
RUL-TAT-01 changes: the Tatkal window still opens 24 hours before
departure, and the same four classes (SL, 3AC, 2AC, CC) offer Tatkal.

## 2. Why

Ujjwal Rail's Tatkal surcharges are revised periodically to reflect
demand on premium last-minute bookings.

## 3. Effective date

This circular takes effect from 2026-10-01 and supersedes the Tatkal
surcharge figures in RUL-TAT-01 §4 from that date onward. Any booking
made before that date keeps the fare it was charged.
