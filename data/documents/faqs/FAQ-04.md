## Q: What details do I need to book a ticket?

You need the train, journey date, class, boarding and destination
stations, and the name and age of each traveller. You do not need to
show ID at booking time — ID is checked on board (see RUL-ID-01 §4).
The assistant will show you a preview of the fare before asking you to
confirm; nothing is booked until you confirm.

Source: RUL-BOOK-01, section "2. Booking lifecycle"; RUL-ID-01, section "4. Who must match the name on the ticket".
