## Q: Mera ticket waitlist mein hai, kya main confirm hone ka wait kar sakta hoon?

Haan, aap wait kar sakte hain. Chart-preparation (departure se 4 ghante
pehle) tak aapki waitlist position confirm ya RAC mein badal sakti
hai. Agar aap chart-preparation tak bhi waitlist mein rehte hain, to
aapko poora refund automatically mil jaata hai — koi cancellation
charge nahi lagta.

Source: RUL-RAC-01, section "3. Waitlisted (WL) tickets and chart preparation".
