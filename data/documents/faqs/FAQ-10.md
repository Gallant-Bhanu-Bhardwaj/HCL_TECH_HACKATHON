## Q: What does RAC mean?

RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) means you and one other RAC
traveller share a berth until the chart is finalised. You may still be
allotted your own berth if one frees up before chart preparation.

Source: RUL-RAC-01, section "2. RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)".
