## Q: What happens to my waitlisted ticket close to departure?

At chart preparation (4 hours before the train's departure from its
origin station), every waitlisted ticket is finalised. If you have
reached a confirmed or RAC position by then, you travel in that
status. If you are still waitlisted at that point, you do not travel,
and you are refunded in full automatically — no cancellation charge.

Source: RUL-RAC-01, section "3. Waitlisted (WL) tickets and chart preparation".
