## Q: Why do fares differ between classes?

Each class (SL, 3AC, 2AC, 1AC, CC, EC, 2S) offers a different level of
comfort, and Ujjwal Rail sets a separate fixed fare for each
class/route combination — see RUL-CLS-01 §2–§3. Fares do not change
with demand or how close to departure you book.

Source: RUL-CLS-01, section "2. Classes offered" and "3. How a fare is set".
