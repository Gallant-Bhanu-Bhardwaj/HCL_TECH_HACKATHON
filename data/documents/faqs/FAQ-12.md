## Q: Will I move up the waitlist if someone else cancels?

Not instantly. Ujjwal Rail does not automatically promote a waitlisted
or RAC traveller the moment another passenger cancels their ticket —
the seat is simply returned to the available pool. Waitlist movement
only happens through Ujjwal Rail's own allocation process before the
chart is prepared.

Source: RUL-RAC-01, section "4. No promotion triggered by someone else's cancellation".
