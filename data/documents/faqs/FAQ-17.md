## Q: Can I bring my pet dog on the train?

Only in a 1AC coupe that you book entirely for yourself, with your dog
inside a secure carrier you bring yourself. Pets are not allowed in
any other class. A trained service animal for a passenger with a
disability is allowed in any class at no extra charge.

Source: RUL-LUG-01, section "4. Pets".
