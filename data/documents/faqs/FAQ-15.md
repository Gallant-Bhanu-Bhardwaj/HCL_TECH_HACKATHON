## Q: Can I change my boarding station after booking?

Yes, to a later stop on the same train and journey, as long as you
ask at least 24 hours before the train's departure from your original
boarding station. The fare you already paid does not change.

Source: RUL-BRD-01, sections "2. What may be changed" and "3. Cutoff".
