## Q: How do I cancel my ticket?

Ask the assistant to cancel your booking. It will show you the exact
refund you will get before anything happens; you then confirm
separately, and the assistant checks and reports the cancellation
afterwards. Nothing is cancelled just by asking — you must confirm.

Source: RUL-CAN-01, section "2. Who may cancel, and when".
