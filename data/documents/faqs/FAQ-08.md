## Q: How is my refund calculated?

Refund = amount paid − cancellation charge, where the charge comes
from the table for your class and how many hours remain before
departure at the time you cancel. It is always computed by the system,
never estimated.

Source: RUL-REF-01, section "2. The refund formula".
