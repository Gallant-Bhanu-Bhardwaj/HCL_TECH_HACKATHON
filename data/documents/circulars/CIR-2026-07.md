## 1. Revised charges

From **15 November 2026**, the 2AC cancellation charge for the "48
hours or more before departure" bracket in RUL-CAN-01 §3 is revised:

| Bracket | Old charge (RUL-CAN-01) | New charge (this circular) |
|---|---|---|
| 48h or more | Rs 150 | **Rs 200** |

This updates the `cancel_charge_2AC_over_48h` setting. No other
class, no other bracket, and no other part of RUL-CAN-01 is affected
by this circular.

## 2. Why

Ujjwal Rail periodically reviews cancellation charges against the cost
of holding a 2AC berth close to departure.

## 3. Effective date and precedence

This circular's Rs 200 figure applies to any cancellation made on or
after 2026-11-15, regardless of when the ticket was originally booked
(R6: the rule in force at the moment of cancellation applies). A
cancellation made before 2026-11-15 used the RUL-CAN-01 figure of Rs
150. See CIR-2026-11 for a further revision of this same bracket,
effective 2026-12-10.
