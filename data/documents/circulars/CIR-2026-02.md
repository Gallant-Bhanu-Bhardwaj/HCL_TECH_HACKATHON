## 1. What this circular adds

This is a seasonal reminder, not a change to RUL-LUG-01: during the
monsoon season (1 June to 30 September), passengers are advised to
pack free-allowance luggage (RUL-LUG-01 §2) in waterproof covers, as
Ujjwal Rail does not compensate for water damage to personal luggage
carried in open racks.

## 2. No rule change

The free luggage allowance figures, the list of disallowed items, and
the pet policy in RUL-LUG-01 are unchanged by this circular. This is
advisory content only, included here as an example of a circular that
does not supersede any rule.
