## 1. Revised charges

From **10 December 2026**, the 2AC cancellation charge for the "48
hours or more before departure" bracket is revised again:

| Bracket | Charge under CIR-2026-07 | New charge (this circular) |
|---|---|---|
| 48h or more | Rs 200 | **Rs 250** |

This updates the `cancel_charge_2AC_over_48h` setting again. No other
class, no other bracket, is affected.

## 2. Effective date — this circular is NOT yet in effect at the time
## this data kit's reference "today" is set

This circular's effective date (2026-12-10) is **after** this data
kit's reference date (2026-12-03). Until 2026-12-10, the 2AC/48h-or-
more charge remains Rs 200 under CIR-2026-07. This circular exists in
the data kit specifically so that:

- A retrieval made with `as_of` before 2026-12-10 must exclude this
  circular's figure from the answer it gives (R6/R11), and
- A judge can `POST /ingest` a similar circular live, or simply move
  the reference date forward, and see the answer change without a
  restart.

## 3. Precedence

Once in effect, this circular supersedes both RUL-CAN-01 §3 and
CIR-2026-07 §1 for the 2AC/48h-or-more bracket only.
