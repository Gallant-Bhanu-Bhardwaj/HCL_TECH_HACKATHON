## 1. Scope

This rule covers what a passenger may bring on board Ujjwal Rail
trains, free of charge and otherwise.

## 2. Free luggage allowance

Each confirmed or RAC traveller may carry, free of charge, personal
luggage up to:

| Class | Free allowance |
|---|---|
| SL, 2S        | 40 kg |
| 3AC, CC       | 35 kg |
| 2AC, EC       | 50 kg |
| 1AC           | 70 kg |

Luggage above this allowance may be booked and charged separately as
parcel luggage at the originating station; this is handled by station
staff, not through the chat assistant.

## 3. Items not allowed

Firearms and ammunition (other than those carried by on-duty
government personnel with valid authorisation), explosive or
inflammable material, corrosive chemicals, and any item that is
illegal to possess are never allowed, in any class, in any amount.

## 4. Pets

A small pet dog or cat may travel with its owner only in a 1AC
coupe/cabin, inside an owner-supplied secure carrier, and only after
the owner has separately booked the entire coupe. Pets are not allowed
in any other class. A service animal accompanying a passenger with a
disability is allowed in any class, at no extra charge, and does not
need a separate booking.

## 5. What the assistant does

The assistant can answer questions from this rule directly; booking a
coupe for a pet, or declaring luggage above the free allowance, is
handled at the station and is outside what the chat assistant can
action.
